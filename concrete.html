Concrete

Building a scalable retail task management platform from 0 to 1, connecting head office, regional managers and store teams through a consistent experience across desktop and tablet.

Overview

Concrete is a retail task management platform connecting head office, regional and district managers with store teams. I led the end-to-end UX/UI redesign, creating a consistent experience across the organisation.

The challenge

The product lacked a visual identity, design system and consistent user flows. Store teams struggled to find and prioritise tasks, while managers couldn’t create tasks or announcements directly from the dashboard. The tablet experience, the primary device used in stores, was broken.

“Balance what customers need with what the business needs.”

Key flows

My role

I shaped the product direction with the Product Manager and Chief Product Officer, balancing customer needs and business goals through an MVP approach.

I aligned executives and stakeholders around the vision, partnered with 6 developers to deliver it, and redesigned core workflows across desktop, tablet and mobile. I validated the experience with HQ and store managers in the UK and US.

I also created Concrete’s brand identity and design system from scratch, covering colour, typography, iconography, components and a responsive grid.

Reflection

This project reinforced the importance of building strong foundations before scaling a product.

Working closely with Product, Engineering and stakeholders taught me how to balance business goals with real user needs, while bringing everyone together around a shared vision.

I’m particularly proud of creating a product experience and design system from scratch, designed to grow alongside the business.
