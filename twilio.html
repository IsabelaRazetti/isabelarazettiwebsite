Staff Product Designer • B2B • 2022–2026

Twilio

I led the strategy and design of a scalable Data Residency experience in Twilio Console, replacing fragmented regional experiences with a shared design pattern adopted across 9+ products.

The challenge

The existing Console duplicated entire product structures for each region, creating a model that wasn’t scalable.

As Twilio expanded into new regions, customers needed a clearer way to understand and manage where their data lived. Meanwhile, product teams lacked a shared design pattern for building regional experiences.

“Start with a per-product MVP, and build towards a global experience.”

My role

I led the strategy and design end to end, partnering closely with Product and Engineering to define the approach, shape the scope and validate solutions through research and usability testing.

When leadership pushed for a global switcher, I didn’t say no; I showed a path. I proposed a phased approach, starting with a per-product MVP and building towards a global experience.

Beyond the core experience, I aligned 9 product teams and 9 designers around a shared pattern, ensuring consistency across the Console. I also mentored a junior designer throughout the project.

The pattern

I documented the regional components as guidelines so every team could build regional experiences the same way — from choosing a region to switching between them.

Reflection

This project reinforced something I strongly believe in: designing complex products is not just about creating the right interface. It’s about understanding the bigger picture, making thoughtful trade-offs and bringing people together around a shared direction.

One of the biggest challenges was balancing the long-term vision with the technical and organisational realities of the product. Rather than pushing for the ideal solution from day one, I learned the importance of creating a clear path forward, breaking down complexity and helping teams move towards a shared vision step by step.

I’m particularly proud of how we turned a fragmented experience into a scalable foundation adopted by multiple product teams.

For me, the biggest impact wasn’t just the experience we shipped, but the alignment, consistency and momentum we created across the organisation.
