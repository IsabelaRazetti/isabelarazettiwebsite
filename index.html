About

There’s more to my story than design.

I’ve lived with epilepsy since I was 15. This experience has shaped my resilience, courage and perspective on life. Something that could have held me back has taught me so much about my own strength and what I’m capable of achieving.

This experience has also shaped the way I connect with people at work. At Twilio, I naturally became someone people could turn to when they felt stuck, overwhelmed or needed support with their career development. I genuinely love mentoring people, helping them find their confidence and creating an environment where everyone can grow.

Outside of product design, I’m building a side project called Mis palabras son tus palabras, where I facilitate peer-support groups for people living with epilepsy.

I’m also a mum of two, a wife and an amateur stand-up comedian. Because I believe life is too interesting to take ourselves too seriously!

I’m a passionate, curious and pragmatic optimist. I love building things, bringing people together and helping others realise what they’re capable of.
